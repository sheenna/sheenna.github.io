systems & software engineer

Hi, I'm Sheenna.

I'm a computer science graduate with too many interests and too little time.

By day, I'm the sole IT engineer for a distribution center in Ontario, CA. I keep an entire warehouse's systems, scanners, and printers running, and I automate the boring parts wherever I can. Before this, I supported a county behavioral health department of over a thousand employees, and before that I managed a technician team at an IT solutions company. Somewhere along the way I became the person people come to when something's broken, or when they want a second pair of eyes on a document. I take pride in both.

I started college as an English major and switched to computer science because I wanted to challenge myself with something I didn't think I was predisposed to. It turns out I love problem solving. The harder the problem and the clearer the goal, the happier I am. I still read like an English major, though (triple digits annually, and yes, I keep count).

I'm a firm believer that everyone has something they're just a little bit of an expert in, something they're excited about. I want to be the person hearing about it, making ideas come to life, and leaving behind documentation that sticks.

When I'm 90, I want to be curled up in a cozy blanket reading webtoons.
